Concept

Not a class. Not a meetup.
A conversation.

授業でも、交流会でもなく、対話の場。

Tokyo is full of brilliant people who rarely sit at the same table — engineers, marketers, doctors, founders, finance professionals from everywhere in the world.

Tokyo Book Dinner brings eight of them together each month around one business book. The book gives us a shared starting point; dinner gives us time to actually talk. You leave with sharper ideas, better English, and people you'll want to see again.

月に一度、一冊のビジネス書を囲んで8名で夕食を。本が対話のきっかけとなり、食事が本音の会話をつくります。

FAQ

Questions

よくある質問

My English isn't perfect. Can I still join? 英語に自信がなくても参加できますか？

Yes. This is not an English test — it's a dinner. Roughly conversational level is enough, and the small table makes it easier, not harder. 完璧な英語は必要ありません。日常会話レベルで十分です。少人数なので、むしろ話しやすい場です。

When do dinners happen? 開催はいつですか？

Every first Friday of the month, 19:30–21:30. If it falls on a holiday, we move to the second Friday and announce at least three weeks ahead. 毎月第1金曜日 19:30–21:30。祝日の場合は第2金曜日に変更し、3週間前までにご案内します。

Do I have to finish the book? 本を読み終えていないとだめですか？

Aim to finish, but if you've read most of it and thought about it, come anyway. Reading in Japanese translation is completely fine. 日本語版での読書も歓迎です。

What's included in ¥8,000? 料金には何が含まれますか？

Dinner course, all-you-can-drink, the reserved private table, and a curated seat — we select each table's mix of industries and backgrounds. The book is not included. コース料理、飲み放題、個室代が含まれます。書籍は各自でご用意ください。

How are seats decided? 席はどのように決まりますか？

We review applications to build a table with a genuine mix — different industries, nationalities, and perspectives. You'll hear from us within 48 hours. 業界や国籍のバランスを考慮して確定し、48時間以内にご連絡します。

What if I need to cancel? キャンセルはできますか？

Full refund up to 72 hours before the dinner. After that we've committed to the restaurant, so no refund — but you can transfer your seat to a friend. 72時間前まで全額返金。それ以降は席のお譲りが可能です。

I'm a Salon member but can't make this month's dinner. サロン会員ですが、今月のディナーに行けません。

You can transfer your seat to a friend (a great way to introduce someone), or roll it over to next month — once per month, no refund. Your weekly sessions continue as usual. お友達への席のお譲り、または翌月への繰り越し（月1回まで）が可能です。週次セッションは通常どおりご参加いただけます。
