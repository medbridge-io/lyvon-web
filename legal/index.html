Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Effective: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by LYVON (“the Company”) on the lyvon.io website and in the MedBridge · MedPath · MedConnect services.

01 General

The Company values your personal information and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and related laws. This policy explains why and how your personal information is processed.

02 Information we collect

① Free trial lesson and consultation requests (website form)

  • Required: name, email, current situation (school year, enrolled/graduated), country or region, target field, self-assessed ability to answer “Why do you want to be a doctor?” in English, planned application, reply language, consent
  • Optional: phone number, preferred days and times, KakaoTalk/LINE ID, English level self-assessment, schools of interest, current preparation, how you heard about LYVON, main concerns, other message; for parents applying for a child: the student’s name and school year; for middle-school students: confirmation that the student is 14 or older or that a parent or guardian is completing the form. MedConnect inquiries: name, email, school and year, message and reply language. LYVON Career inquiries: name, email, target country and role, message and reply language.
  • Recorded with a submitted form: the page the form was sent from, the site you came from (domain only), campaign tags (utm) and the first page of your visit. This does not identify you and is used only to understand how inquiries reach us.

② Free Welcome Guide requests

  • Required: name, email, guide language, consent

③ Enrolment and use of the services

  • Name, contact details (phone, email), student information (school year, goals, etc.), parent contact details (when the parent dashboard is used)
  • Contract and payment information

④ Information created while you use the services

  • Lesson attendance records
  • Learning data (LLR reports, speaking and feedback data)
  • Access logs, IP address, browser and device information, cookies (see Article 09)

03 Purposes of use

  • Receiving free trial lesson and consultation requests, scheduling, and replying in your chosen language
  • Sending the Free Welcome Guide
  • Running and managing lessons
  • Personalised learning (MedBridge / MedPath / MedConnect)
  • Creating and providing the LLR (Lesson Learned Report), including the parent dashboard
  • Consultation and customer support
  • Improving the service (aggregate statistics that do not identify individuals)
  • Preventing misuse and spam

04 Retention

Personal information is destroyed without delay once its purpose is fulfilled, except for the items below.

ItemRetention
Free trial lesson and consultation requests1 year from the request (converted to enrolment records if you enrol)
Free Welcome Guide requests1 year from the request
Contract and payment information5 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
Consumer complaints and dispute records3 years (same Act)
Consultation records1 year
Learning data (LLR)1 year after the service ends
Website access records3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

If you ask us to delete your information, we delete it without delay, except where the law requires us to keep it. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded.

05 Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide your personal information to third parties, except:

  • with your prior consent
  • when required by law

The minimum information shared with instructors and our teaching partner to run lessons is covered by Article 06 (outsourcing) and Article 07 (transfer abroad).

08 Your rights and those of legal guardians

  • You can ask at any time, by writing to hello@lyvon.io, to access, correct or delete your information, to stop its processing, or to withdraw consent. We act without delay (within 10 days).
  • You may refuse consent to the collection and use of your information. Without consent to the required items, however, we cannot process the request (trial lesson, guide, etc.).
  • For children under 14, we process personal information with the consent of a legal guardian, and the guardian fills in the form. Guardians can exercise the rights above on the child's behalf.

09 Cookies

  • The website itself sets a single cookie (lv_lang) that remembers the language you choose. It is deleted when you close your browser.
  • We use Naver Analytics (NAVER Corp.) to understand visits to the site. Naver may use cookies for this, and access information such as the pages visited, the referring site, browser and device information and IP address is sent to Naver. We do not use advertising cookies.
  • We use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how visitors use the site. It sets its own cookies (_ga), and access information such as the pages visited, the referring site, browser and device information and approximate location is sent to Google. We do not use its advertising features.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings; your language choice may then not be kept.
  • To see how inquiries reach us, the site you came from (domain only), campaign tags and the first page of your visit are kept in your browser’s temporary storage (sessionStorage) and deleted when you close the tab. They are sent to us only if you submit a form.

10 Security measures

  • An internal management plan for personal information
  • Access limited to the minimum number of people who need it
  • Two-step verification and access-rights management
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Keeping and regularly reviewing access records

11 Privacy officer and remedies

  • Privacy officer: Patrick Noh, Representative
  • Email: hello@lyvon.io

You can also report or seek advice on privacy violations from the following Korean bodies.

  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center: privacy.kisa.or.kr / 118 (in Korea)
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: www.kopico.go.kr / 1833-6972
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office: www.spo.go.kr / 1301 (in Korea)
  • Korean National Police Agency: ecrm.police.go.kr / 182 (in Korea)

12 Changes

We announce changes to this policy on the website at least 7 days before they take effect, and at least 30 days before for material changes to your rights.

Terms of Service

These terms set the conditions for using LYVON's MedBridge · MedPath · MedConnect services.

01 Purpose

These terms set out the rights, obligations and responsibilities of LYVON (“the Company”) and users in relation to the MedBridge, MedPath and MedConnect services.

02 Services

The Company provides the following services.

  • MedBridge — foundation program in medical English (an introduction to English oral exams and interviews)
  • MedPath — preparation for English oral exams and interviews at overseas medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary schools (Starter · Core · Pro)
  • MedConnect — medical English after admission (for medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary students and future healthcare professionals)
  • LLR system — reports based on learning data

03 Using the services

  • Users can apply for the services through the procedure the Company provides.
  • The Company may limit or decline an application for a reasonable cause, such as being unable to run the lessons, and will explain why.

04 Lesson policy

  • Lessons run by advance booking.
  • Schedule changes must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Same-day cancellations and no-shows may be counted as lessons taken.

05 Payment and refunds

Fees are paid before the service starts. Refunds follow the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and the tuition refund standard of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on the Establishment and Operation of Private Teaching Institutes (Korea).

WhenRefund
Within 7 days of payment, before the first lesson (cancellation)Full
Before the first lessonFull
Before one third of the lessons are takenTwo thirds of the amount paid
Before half of the lessons are takenHalf of the amount paid
After half of the lessons are takenNo refund
  • For courses longer than one month, the month in which the refund is requested is refunded by the table above and the remaining months in full. One month means the programme's monthly number of lessons.
  • If the Company cannot run the lessons, the remaining lessons are refunded in full.
  • Refunds are made within 3 business days of the request, to the original payment method or account.

06 User obligations

Users must not:

  • use another person's information
  • disrupt the service
  • record lessons or distribute materials without permission
  • behave inappropriately towards instructors or the service

07 Intellectual property

  • Copyright in all teaching content and materials (including MedBridge · MedPath · MedConnect) belongs to LYVON.
  • Users may not copy or distribute them without permission.

08 Changes to and suspension of the service

The Company may change or suspend the service for system maintenance, service improvements or unavoidable operational reasons, and will give advance notice. Lessons that cannot be provided as a result are refunded under Article 05.

09 Limitation of liability

Unless it acted intentionally or negligently, the Company is not liable for:

  • problems caused by the user
  • service disruption caused by external factors (networks, etc.)

10 Disputes

  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.
  • Disputes are resolved by the competent court under the Korean Civil Procedure Act.
