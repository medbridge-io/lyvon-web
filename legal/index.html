Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy · Effective: launch date of the new website (2027 edition)

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by LYVON (“the Company”) on the lyvon.io website and in the MedBridge · MedPath · MedConnect services. The Korean version is the official text; this translation is provided for reference.

01 General

The Company values your personal information and complies with Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act and related laws. This policy explains for what purposes and in what ways your personal information is processed.

02 Information we collect

① When you request a free lesson or consultation (website form)

  • Required: name, email, current situation (school year, enrolled/graduated, etc.), country/region of residence, reply language, consent to collection and use of personal information
  • Optional: phone number, target field, schools of interest, answer to “why medicine”, English level (self-assessed), main concerns, planned application timing, current preparation, monthly budget range, how you heard about LYVON

② When you enroll and use the services

  • Name, contact details (phone, email), student information (school year, goals, etc.), parent contact details (when a parent account is used)
  • Contract and payment information

③ Information generated while you use the services

  • Lesson and session attendance records
  • Learning data (LLR reports, speaking and feedback data)
  • Access logs, IP address, browser and device information, cookies (see 09)

03 Purposes of collection and use

  • Receiving free lesson and consultation requests, scheduling, and replying in your chosen language
  • Running and managing lessons and sessions
  • Providing personalised learning (MedBridge / MedPath / MedConnect programs)
  • Creating and providing LLR (Lesson Learned Report), including through parent accounts
  • Consultation and customer support
  • Improving the services and website usage statistics (analytics cookies only with consent)
  • Preventing misuse and spam

04 Retention period

Personal information is destroyed without delay once the purpose of collection and use is achieved, except in the following cases.

ItemRetention
Free lesson and consultation requests1 year from the request (converted to enrollment records if you enroll)
Contract and payment information5 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
Consumer complaints and dispute records3 years (same Act)
Consultation records1 year
Learning data (LLR)1 year after the service ends
Website access records3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded.

05 Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide your personal information to outside parties, except:

  • where you have given prior consent
  • where required by law

The minimum information shared with instructors and education partners to run lessons is governed by 06 (outsourcing) and 07 (overseas transfer).

08 Your rights and those of legal guardians

  • You may at any time ask to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@lyvon.io. The Company acts without delay (within 10 days).
  • Personal information of children under 14 is processed with the consent of a legal guardian, and the form is filled in by the guardian. Guardians may exercise the rights above on the child’s behalf.

09 Cookies and website analytics

  • By default the website uses only cookies needed for basic functions such as language settings.
  • Visit statistics are collected with cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics; Google Analytics cookies are used only if you consent.
  • You can refuse cookies in the cookie banner or your browser settings.

10 Security measures

  • Access to personal information is limited to the minimum number of people who need it
  • Two-step verification and access control on accounts
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)

  • Email: hello@lyvon.io

12 Changes to this policy

Changes are posted on the website at least 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days before for changes that materially affect your rights.
