About LYVON

What is LYVON?

LYVON provides 1:1 online English speaking training for overseas medical school oral exams and interviews, starting with Hungarian schools of medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and veterinary medicine. Lessons are live, 50 minutes long and held 2–4 times a week. LYVON works from Seoul, Tokyo and Manila.

Who is LYVON for?

Students preparing for the English oral entrance exams and interviews of Hungarian medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary schools, learners who first need a foundation in medical English, and students who have already been admitted and want to keep training their medical English.

Which universities does LYVON prepare students for?

MedPath currently focuses on the English oral entrance exams of Hungarian schools: Semmelweis University, the University of Szeged, the University of Pécs and the University of Debrecen, as well as the University of Veterinary Medicine Budapest.

What results have LYVON students achieved?

22 of the 24 LYVON students who sat entrance exams in 2025–2026 were admitted (11 of 11 in 2025, 11 of 13 in 2026): Semmelweis 12, Szeged 6, Pécs 3 and the University of Veterinary Medicine Budapest 1. Students who stopped before the exam or moved their exam to a later year are not counted. Past results do not predict individual outcomes.

Is LYVON an admissions agency?

No. LYVON provides English education only. It does not handle applications, does not act as an admissions agent and does not guarantee admission. An agency and LYVON do different jobs, and students can use both.

Programs

What is the difference between MedBridge, MedPath and MedConnect?

MedBridge is the foundation: 30 topics of medical English vocabulary, pronunciation and sentence structure over about 3 months. MedPath is the flagship oral exam preparation: 50 biology and chemistry lessons taken through three tiers. MedConnect is a post-admission membership for medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary students, running as a pilot since July 2026.

Which program should I start with?

If explaining science in English is still hard, start with MedBridge. If you are applying within the next 12 months, start with MedPath. If your exam is within 6 months, ask for an intensive MedPath plan. If you are already admitted, MedConnect. The free lesson includes a diagnostic that tells you where to start.

How long does preparation take?

At 4 sessions a week, MedBridge takes about 3 months and each MedPath tier (Starter, Core, Pro) takes about 3 months. The full pathway is about 12 months. With fewer sessions a week it takes longer; the plan is set after the free diagnostic.

My exam is less than six months away. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. After the free diagnostic, LYVON builds an intensive plan around the exam date and the topics that matter most.

What do MedPath lessons cover?

MedPath is built on the oral exam topics Semmelweis publishes: 27 biology lessons and 23 chemistry lessons. The same 50 lessons are taken three levels deeper: Define (Starter), Explain (Core) and Justify (Pro), so students learn to answer a professor's follow-up questions in English.

If I already know biology and chemistry, why do I need speaking practice?

The oral exam does not only test what you know. It tests whether you can define and explain it in English, out loud, under follow-up questions. Most students fall short not because they know too little but because they cannot explain what they know in English. LYVON trains that output.

Lessons

How are lessons held?

Live 1:1 video lessons on Zoom, 50 minutes each, 2–4 times a week. Students choose the days and times.

What are the lesson hours?

Weekdays 09:00–22:50 and weekends 09:00–17:50, Korea/Japan time (one hour earlier in Taiwan and Hong Kong). Lessons start on the hour.

Who teaches the lessons?

Professional English instructors from LYVON's partner StepForward (Philippines), trained in the LYVON method. Lessons run in English.

How is progress tracked?

After every session the instructor issues a Lesson Learned Report (LLR) across five areas: progress, speaking, listening, vocabulary and corrections. Error patterns are tracked every one to two weeks, detailed feedback is given every four sessions, and students and parents each have their own dashboard.

Fees & enrolment

Why isn't the price on the website?

Fees depend on the starting level and the number of sessions a week. After the free lesson, each family receives a written diagnostic with a suggested plan and its exact fee. There is no obligation to enrol.

Is there a free trial lesson?

Yes. The free trial is a live 50-minute 1:1 lesson in the same format as a regular session, followed by a written diagnostic and a suggested plan. Book it here.

Can I reschedule a lesson?

Yes, if you tell LYVON at least 24 hours before the lesson. Details are in the Terms.
