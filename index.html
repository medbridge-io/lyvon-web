01 — OUR PHILOSOPHY

Connected Across Asia. Educating Beyond Borders.

At LYVON, we believe meaningful opportunities should not be limited by geography. We connect ambitious individuals with the knowledge, preparation, and support they need to pursue education and careers across borders.

We train output, not input.

Most students don’t fall short because they know too little. They fall short because they can’t explain what they know — in English, under questioning. LYVON trains that ability, one student at a time.

04 — THE LYVON METHOD

A system, not a tutor.

LYVON is not an admissions agency. Agencies handle applications; we build the ability to pass the exam — with a method that is designed, recorded and measured, session after session.

THREE LEVELS · THE SAME 50 LESSONS, TAKEN DEEPER

  1. 01 · STARTER DEFINE “What is it?” — precise definitions in clear English.
  2. 02 · CORE EXPLAIN “How does it work?” — structured explanation of mechanisms.
  3. 03 · PRO JUSTIFY “Why does it matter?” — reasoning that holds up under follow-up questions.

EVIDENCE OF PROGRESS

Not a feeling — a record.

After every session, the instructor issues a Lesson Learned Report (LLR) across five areas. Reports accumulate into a program debrief: what was solved, what remains, and what comes next.

  • Progress · Speaking · Listening · Vocabulary · Corrections
  • Error-pattern tracking every one to two weeks
  • Detailed feedback every four sessions
