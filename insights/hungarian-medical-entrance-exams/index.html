Hungarian medical entrance exams: how each school tests you

November 2026 · Medical Admissions

Entrance exams for the English-language programmes at Hungarian medical schools are not a single national test taken on one day. Each school runs several exam dates from spring to early summer, and an online written test is followed by an interview in English with faculty.

How the four schools are structured

SchoolWrittenOral / interviewWhen
Semmelweis (Budapest)Biology, chemistry, general English and medical English: 20 questions each, 20 minutes per subjectMotivation and English communication, plus an oral exam in biology and chemistrySpring to the end of June
Szeged60 questions in 60 minutes (20 each in biology, chemistry and English)20 minutes on Zoom: conversation plus two biology and two chemistry questionsEvery two weeks, late February to late June
Pécs100 minutes (biology 20, chemistry 20, general English 40, medical English 20)A 20–30 minute interview with facultyEvery Wednesday; applications 1 November to 30 June
University of Veterinary Medicine BudapestBiology, chemistry and English (50% of the score)Motivation and academic background, plus biology and chemistry (50%)March to July

The gap the interview does not close

Semmelweis tests motivation, English communication and oral biology and chemistry together, and states that knowledge of English terminology is required in every area. The veterinary university weights the written and oral parts 50:50. An answer you got right on paper does not turn into points unless you can explain it in front of the examiners.

Dates and preparatory courses

  • Semmelweis, entry 2027: the application deadline is 31 May 2027. The opening date has not been announced yet.
  • Preparatory years: Szeged offers an eight-month Foundation Year; Pécs offers 5-, 10- and 15-month language preparation courses. Semmelweis has no separate preparatory year, only a 10-day intensive course before entry.

Where LYVON fits

LYVON does not handle applications and does not guarantee admission. What we train is the ability to explain what you know, in English, in the interview room. Formats and dates change every year, so always check each school’s official page before you apply.
