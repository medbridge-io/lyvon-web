Studying medicine in English: an exam culture you prove out loud

November 2026 · Medical School

Most students in Korea and Japan are used to multiple-choice and written exams. The first difference they meet at a Hungarian medical school is the format of the exam itself.

Exams are conversations, not paper

Semmelweis’s study regulations define exams as oral, written, practical or combined, and comprehensive exams are taken before a committee. The final state exam also has an oral part. Grades are decided less by picking the right answer than by carrying your reasoning in English through a professor’s follow-up questions.

This culture starts with the entrance exam. Semmelweis, Szeged, Pécs and the veterinary university in Budapest all follow the written test with an interview in English.

Six years of exams you answer out loud, in English

  • Years 1⁠–⁠3: Comprehensive exams run from semester 2 to semester 6: anatomy, biophysics, biochemistry, physiology, cell biology, genetics and immunology, microbiology, pharmacology, pathology and pathophysiology. Each one gathers a year or more of a subject into a single exam in front of the examiners.
  • Year 6: After clinical rotations in internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics and other specialties, the degree ends with the oral part of the final examination and the thesis defence.

In other words, the entrance interview is only the beginning. For six years, the ability to explain in English shapes your grades. Building that habit before you start makes every exam period that follows lighter.

A large university, a diverse class

Of Semmelweis’s roughly 12,000 students, more than a third are international students from about 100 countries (2021/22). Discussions with classmates and group presentations all happen in English.

How LYVON sees it

Every MedBridge and MedPath lesson ends with practice explaining what you learned out loud and answering follow-up questions. That prepares you for the entrance exam and starts the habit early.

After admission, MedConnect takes over. It is a membership for medical, dental, pharmacy and veterinary students, built around discussion, journal club and case presentations, so you can keep preparing in English for six years of oral exams and presentations. It has been running as a pilot since July 2026.
