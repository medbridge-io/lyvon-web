01 — THE GAP

You know the science. Explaining it in English is another skill.

  1. 01

    Words, but no sentences

    Terms are memorised, but can’t be explained in one clear English sentence.

  2. 02

    Freezing mid‑answer

    What flows in your first language stops the moment it has to be said in English.

  3. 03

    Biology and chemistry in silos

    Isolated facts collapse under a follow-up question.

  4. 04

    No speaking practice

    Few chances to explain something out loud, start to finish.

02 — OUTPUT, NOT INPUT

Not memorising. Speaking.

MedBridge gives students two simple frameworks and makes them use them out loud, every session, until they become automatic.

05 — FAQ

Questions families ask.

Why isn’t the price on the website?

Fees depend on where you start and how many sessions a week you take. After the free lesson you receive a written diagnostic with a suggested plan and its exact fee. There is no obligation to enrol.

How are lessons held?

Live 1:1 video lessons on Zoom, 50 minutes each, 2–4 times a week. You choose the days and times.

Who teaches the lessons?

Professional English instructors from our partner StepForward (Philippines), trained in the LYVON method. Lessons run in English; LYVON talks with students and families in Korean, Japanese or English.

What are the lesson hours?

Weekdays 09:00–22:50 and weekends 09:00–17:50, Korea/Japan time — one hour earlier in Taiwan and Hong Kong.

My exam is less than six months away. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. After the free diagnostic we build a shorter plan around your exam date and the topics that matter most.

Can I reschedule a lesson?

Yes — tell us at least 24 hours before the lesson. The details are in our Terms.

Do you handle applications?

No. LYVON provides English education only. We do not act as an admissions agent and do not guarantee admission.
