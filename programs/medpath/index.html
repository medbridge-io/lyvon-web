01 — THE ORAL EXAM

Knowing the science is not enough.

In the English oral entrance exam, examiners don’t only check whether an answer is right. They listen to how a candidate explains it — and what happens under follow-up questions.

  1. 01

    Pronunciation

    Stress of medical terms and sentence rhythm.

02 — THREE TIERS

One lesson, three depths.

MedPath doesn’t keep piling on new content. Students return to the same 50 lessons at a higher level of thinking each time — so unexpected follow-up questions don’t break their answers.

  1. TIER 1 · STARTER · 3 MONTHS DEFINE “What is it?”

    EXAMPLE · PROTEINS
    Define a protein and its four levels of structure.

  2. TIER 2 · CORE · 3 MONTHS EXPLAIN “How does it work?”

    EXAMPLE · PROTEINS
    Explain how a protein folds into its functional shape.

  3. TIER 3 · PRO · 3 MONTHS JUSTIFY “Why does it matter?”

    EXAMPLE · PROTEINS
    Argue why a single amino-acid change can cause disease.

Examples are illustrative. Each tier runs 52 sessions: 50 lessons plus one full review each for biology and chemistry.
